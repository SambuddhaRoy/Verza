<p>All of YouTube Music. No ads. Sign in to bring your likes, playlists and Home, or skip it. Nothing is tracked.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>No ads.</b> All of YouTube Music, full length, in the background and on your lock screen.</li>
<li><b>3D covers.</b> Tilt your phone in Now Playing and the cover moves in 3D. Android 13 and up.</li>
<li><b>Lossless downloads.</b> Save songs as FLAC, as good as the original recording.</li>
<li><b>Lyrics in time.</b> Follow along, line by line.</li>
<li><b>Made for you.</b> Home, mixes and Discovery radio, built from what you play.</li>
<li><b>Your own music.</b> Songs already on your phone play right alongside everything else.</li>
<li><b>Flick it over.</b> Flick your phone toward your computer and the song moves there.</li>
<li><b>Android Auto.</b> Your library and queue on your car's screen.</li>
<li><b>And more.</b> An equaliser, sleep timer, skip silence, widgets, a quick-settings tile, and two themes.</li>
